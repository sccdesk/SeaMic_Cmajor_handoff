M5 — The 2-second boot gate silenced every decision Symptom. An enter-only probe (floor + SNR + enter latch, nothing else) rendered complete silence for the whole 7 s file, even though hand-calculation said SNR ≫ threshold during the loud segment. Removing a single guard — if (initSamp >= initLen) with initLen = 2 s wrapping the enter decision — made the probe sing on the next render.
Root cause. Layered bookkeeping: the sample counter incremented inside a branch that other logic (rotation) also gated; a decision freeze that outlived its usefulness once the floor boot was shortened; and a gate depending on per-sample counters across block-based rendering is an extra failure surface for zero benefit once the floor starts at a sane cap.
Fix. Floor boot kept but shortened to 0.25 s (instant-min converges within one 32 ms window); all decision gating removed — enter runs from sample 0, safe because the cap-booted floor (M3) guarantees SNR ≤ 0 dB on boot noise.
Verification. Enter-probe and flag-beep probes both open during speech segments (seg2/4/5 at full beep level −9.03 dBFS).
M6 — Gate/AGC interlock: pre-roll pump and post-speech noise pumping Symptom. (a) With the gate initial open and the AGC free to adapt before any speech, the chain amplified the quiet opening seconds. (b) After speech, a lingering VAD kept the AGC "unfrozen" into the following noise, which it dutifully rode toward the −19 dBFS target — noise pumping, the exact artefact the product wanted to eliminate. (c) The legacy 1 s shutoff hysteresis (pre-project) broadcast noise tails for a full second.
Fix. Three coordinated changes: gate starts closed (att = 10 dB), opening only on first detection; the AGC gains a speechSeen latch (no adaptation before the first utterance); VAD hardening (flatness exit + cap-booted floor) stops the flag lingering on stationary noise — while the requested long hold was implemented where it belongs: as audio-tail hold (+400 ms) rather than as AGC-adaptation time.
Verification. Final renders: opening noise silent (−120 dBFS measured), post-speech noise suppressed relative to input; documented residual leak ≈ 1 s (§7).
M7 — The analyzer read float32 WAVs as int16 (measurement invalidated a full round of conclusions) Symptom. Probe numbers were internally contradictory: a "lower-only" floor appeared to rise; an unconditional pass-probe was black for one second; the K-weighted output appeared input-independent at ≈ −41 dBFS; peaks pinned at exactly 1.000000 across unrelated patches.
Probe → root cause. The bisect reached the instruments themselves. Dumping the rendered file header (dumpwav) revealed fmt = 0xFFFE (WAVE_FORMAT_EXTENSIBLE), 32-bit float, stereo, while stimulus files were fmt = 1, 16-bit PCM. The analyzer chose its decoder by testing for format 3 (IEEE_FLOAT) — so every float32 sample was parsed as two int16 samples (low mantissa word first), producing structured nonsense that correlated with signal magnitude just enough to look plausible.
Fix. Decode by bits: 16 → int16, otherwise float32.
Consequence. All historical measurements were re-run and the verdict flipped: with fixed instrumentation the VAD was largely working — flag-beep open through speech, partial during hold windows (§7). Two earlier "root causes" were partly artifacts; the report keeps only the independently evidenced core of M3 (phantom SNR from the independent vadprobe tool).
LessonInstrumentation is part of the system under test. When a measurement contradicts the code's own invariants (a lower-only floor rising), suspect the measurement before the model.
